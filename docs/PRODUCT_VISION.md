# Product Vision

## Name

**LegalOS AI** — The Legal Intelligence Operating System for UK immigration, protection, employment status, and access to justice.

## What we are not

We are **not** an AI solicitor. We never claim:

> “I am your lawyer.”

## What we are

We help people and organisations:

- understand legal situations,
- prepare evidence,
- organise documents,
- work with qualified legal professionals.

## Core vertical (v1)

**UK Immigration & Protection OS**

Student / Graduate / Skilled Worker / Sponsor / eVisa / RTW / ILR / Citizenship / Asylum / HP / NRM / VTS / Family / Appeals / JR prep.

## Differentiation

| Platform     | Focus                                                                                                     |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Harvey       | Legal professionals                                                                                       |
| Norm AI      | Regulatory / agentic law at institutions                                                                  |
| Form fillers | Narrow citizen tools                                                                                      |
| **LegalOS**  | Multilingual access to justice + immigration expertise + evidence orchestration + solicitor collaboration |

## Trust posture

We do not take trust lightly. We aim to be **transparent** and **trustworthy**. Nothing is guaranteed. Credibility is earned by work quality and platform metrics.

## End state

A Legal Intelligence Operating System that reduces administrative burden, improves access to justice, and lets professionals focus on complex advocacy.
